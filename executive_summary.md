# Executive Summary – Churn Analytics (Capstone brief)

**Headline:** 46.7% of customers churned, costing **$409.93 MRR (32.3%)**. Every churned customer was on a month-to-month plan.

1. **Where:** Month-to-month customers churn 7/7; annual and two-year customers 0/8.
2. **When:** Churners left after ~7 months on average (retained customers: ~29 months).
3. **Warning sign:** Churners raised 4.3 support tickets on average vs 1.0.
4. **Exposure:** $99.98 MRR (2 active customers) is Medium-risk today.
5. **Action:** Annual-plan incentive in the first 60 days, a support-ticket trigger, and a monthly re-score.
6. **Impact (illustrative):** converting half of would-be churners ≈ $205 MRR (≈ $2,460/yr) retained.
7. **Caution:** only 15 customers, in-sample validation, and contract is perfectly correlated with churn here, so treat these as hypotheses to test on a larger dataset.

See `memo/recommendation_memo.md` for details, `index.html` for the dashboard, and `notebooks/` for the method.
